How to present this model

“I enter the values of A, B, C, D and E, then click Calculate Result to get the answer and see each calculation step. My construction approach uses postfix notation and a stack. Operands are pushed onto the stack; when an operator appears, two subtrees are popped and connected under that operator. The final tree represents the given expression.”
